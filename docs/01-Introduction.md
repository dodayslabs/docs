# Introduction

Hi there, welcome to the DoDays API.

Our API enables users to authenticate and access their Family account at [www.dodays.co.uk](https://www.dodays.co.uk).

It's also a public API, meaning you can get started with [Authentication](/02-authentication.md) and generate your first API token.

If there's anything you need please reach out to our engineering team at engineering@dodays.co.uk.
